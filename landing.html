About Us

TrackIt is simple expense-tracking app built to help you keep up with your income and expenses, understand your spending without making money management feel like work. Whether it's your salary, a side income, a quick transfer, your daily spending, or a shared expense, TrackIt gives you one place to keep track of it all. We're not here to tell you how to spend your money, We're here to help you understand it. When you know what comes in, what goes out, and where your money is going, it's easier to make better decisions about what to do next.

Our goal is simple, we want to make keeping track of your money something you can actually stick with. No complicated spreadsheets, No confusing financial terms, No trying to remember everything in your head, Just a simple way to keep an eye on your money. TrackIt is about awareness, not perfection. You don't have to get everything right. You just need to start paying attention.
